What Sets Us Apart

Clean Ingredients

Every bar is made from whole, unprocessed ingredients you can actually pronounce. No artificial flavours, colours, or preservatives — just real food that nourishes your body.

Sweetened with Dates

We sweeten our bars with real whole dates — never refined sugar or artificial sweeteners. That means you get nature’s rich, caramel-like sweetness along with the natural fibre found in dates. This fibre helps slow the release of sugars, supporting steadier energy and helping avoid sudden spikes. It’s not sugar extracted from dates — it’s the wholesome goodness of whole dates in every bite.

Control insulin spikes

Our bars are designed with a low glycaemic profile so you get sustained energy without the crash. Perfect for steady focus and balanced blood sugar throughout the day.

Gluten Free

Made with sattu powder from dry-roasted Bengal gram, also known as kala chana, our bars are naturally gluten free and packed with wholesome goodness. The chickpeas are roasted, their outer husks are removed, and they are then finely ground into a nourishing flour that brings a rich, earthy flavor and satisfying texture — without any wheat or gluten-containing grains. A delicious choice for anyone looking for a clean, gluten-free snack.

Made with Ghee (Clarified Butter)

We use traditional ghee for its rich flavour and health benefits — packed with fat-soluble vitamins, easy to digest, and naturally free from lactose and casein.

Healthy Snack for Kids

Safe, nutritious, and kid-approved. Our bars make the perfect lunchbox addition — giving children the energy they need without any of the junk.
