What Sets Us Apart

Clean Ingredients

Every bar is made from whole, unprocessed ingredients you can actually pronounce. No artificial flavours, colours, or preservatives — just real food that nourishes your body.

Sweetened with Dates

Sweetened only with real whole dates—never refined sugar or artificial sweeteners. Dates provide rich, caramel-like sweetness along with natural fiber, helping support a steadier release of energy.

Gluten Free

Made with sattu from dry-roasted Bengal gram (kala chana), our bars are naturally gluten-free, with a rich, earthy flavor and satisfying texture — no wheat or gluten-containing grains.

Made with Ghee (Clarified Butter)

We use traditional ghee for its rich flavour and health benefits — packed with fat-soluble vitamins, easy to digest, and naturally free from lactose and casein.

Healthy Snack for Kids

Safe, nutritious, and kid-approved. Our bars make the perfect lunchbox addition — giving children the energy they need without any of the junk.
