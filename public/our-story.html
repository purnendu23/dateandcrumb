Made for busy days.
With ingredients you can trust.

As a twin mom, I was always searching the snack shelves for something I could truly feel good about — for my kids, for myself, for busy mornings, work bags, lunchboxes, long shifts, and the little moments between meals.

But so many snacks that looked wholesome still had added sugars, syrups, artificial sweeteners, or ingredients I would not normally use at home.

That search became the beginning of Date & Crumb.

Our bars are inspired by the warmth of familiar pantry ingredients and Indian flavors, made with whole dates, roasted gram flour, nuts, ghee, and simple, recognizable ingredients.

They are made for everyday life — convenient enough for busy days, comforting enough to feel homemade, and honest enough to trust.

Simple ingredients. Honest goodness.
